# Arabie.ai Social Media Content Templates

## 🎯 Purpose
These templates help create consistent, inclusive, and engaging public posts about Arabie.ai across social platforms.

## 📋 Content Guidelines

### Core Messaging Principles
- **Inclusive Language**: Use "we" and "our community" to foster belonging
- **Cultural Sensitivity**: Acknowledge Arabic-speaking audiences globally
- **Value-First**: Lead with the benefit to users
- **Accessible**: Simple language that translates well

### Key Messages to Include
- Democratizing AI for Arabic speakers
- Building bridges between technology and culture
- Empowering Arabic content creators and learners
- Community-driven innovation

## ✍️ Content Creation Checklist

Before posting, ensure:
- [ ] Message is clear and valuable
- [ ] Includes both Arabic and English where appropriate
- [ ] Uses inclusive pronouns (we, our, together)
- [ ] Has a clear call-to-action
- [ ] Includes relevant hashtags
- [ ] Respects cultural sensitivities
- [ ] Encourages community engagement

## 🎨 Visual Content Guidelines

- Use brand colors consistently
- Include Arabic text in graphics
- Ensure text is readable on mobile
- Represent diverse users in imagery
- Keep designs clean and professional

## 📅 Posting Schedule Suggestions

**LinkedIn**: 2-3 times per week
- Tuesday: Educational content
- Thursday: Feature updates
- Saturday: Community spotlights

**Twitter/X**: Daily
- Mix of original content and community engagement

**Instagram**: 3-4 times per week
- Focus on visual storytelling
- Behind-the-scenes content
- User testimonials

## 💬 Engagement Phrases

**For encouraging participation:**
- "نريد أن نسمع منك" (We want to hear from you)
- "Share your experience with..."
- "What would you like to see next?"
- "Join our growing community..."

**For celebrating milestones:**
- "Thanks to our amazing community..."
- "Together, we've reached..."
- "Your support makes this possible..."

Remember: Every post is an opportunity to make someone feel included in the Arabie.ai journey! 🚀
